# Today S01 S02 D01

S01/S02/D01. FR-06. AT-04. Demo routine with an optional block.

## 1. Wi-Fi
1. Open Today. Tap Short. The optional block is struck through with "—" minutes.
2. Button reads "Start N-min session". "Why this trade" names kept and left-out blocks.
3. Tap it. Only the kept blocks run; the left-out one never shows.
4. End. Done says "Short plan · Kept …. Left out … today." and that your full plan is unchanged.
5. Back on Today, tap Full. Start: every block runs, as before.
6. Week strip shows your real sessions.
7. Start a session, log a set, go to Today. Card "Resume session · N min in", minutes right.
8. Tap "Finish here". The session finishes.

## 2. Airplane mode
9. Airplane on. On Today, pick Short and start. Log a set. Header says "Saved on this device".
10. Week strip says "Not yet known", not a made-up number.
11. Airplane off. It turns "Synced", with one set only.

## 3. Lock
12. Leave a session open, lock two minutes, unlock. Today's Resume minutes are right.

## 4. Kill the tab
13. Close the tab, reopen Today. Resume card still there; sets kept.

## 5. 320 px and wide
14. Zoom to 320 px. Resume label stays on one line, no sideways scroll.
15. Open on a desktop at 1024 px or wider: two columns.

## 6. Night Gym
16. Dark mode. Today, struck-through block and Resume card follow.
