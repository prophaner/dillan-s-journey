# Profile and Settings

Profile and Settings shells. FR-01, FR-04, FR-09, FR-17. Demo account.

## 1. Wi-Fi
1. Open Profile. All six sections are listed.
2. Untick "Available" on one item. It reads "Not available". Open Today and Train: that gear is not used (if shown).
3. Type "Kettlebell 16 kg", tap Add.
4. Tap its bin. A toast says "Kettlebell 16 kg removed" with Undo. Tap Undo. Known issue: gear that never synced may not come back. Either result is fine.
5. On a limitation, tap "Resolved". The tag changes. Type a note, tap outside. The note stays.
6. Set Days per week, tap training days, pick Typical session, change "Week starts on".
7. Pick an Experience chip.
8. In Profile Units, choose Kilograms (kg). Open Progress: numbers convert on screen. 
9. Pick a time zone.

## 2. Airplane mode
10. Airplane on. Untick one gear item. Open the menu (phone) or look at the sidebar (desktop): it says "Saved on this device".
11. Airplane off. It turns "Synced", once.

## 3. Settings
12. Open Settings, then "Your data on this phone". The sheet opens.
13. Units here: known issue, it may flip back after sync. Use Profile.
14. With airplane on and an unsynced change, tap Sign out. It warns the items haven't synced. Cancel.
15. Six cards say "Coming later": Notifications, Privacy, AI data permissions, Export, Deletion, Accessibility.

## 4. Kill the tab
16. Close the tab, reopen Profile. Every change is still there.

## 5. 320 px
17. Zoom to 320 px. Profile and Settings: no sideways scroll.
