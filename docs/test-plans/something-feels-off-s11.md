# Something feels off S11

S11. AT-05. Demo routine; block 2 is a superset.

## 1. Wi-Fi
1. Set screen: tap "Something feels off". Sheet "Stop or adjust. Your call." and "Your sets so far are saved."
2. Close it (Keep going). Log a set, let rest start. Tap it again: same sheet, one tap.
3. In the superset, open it mid-round: same sheet.
4. Mid-exercise with two sets done: under "Where?" choose an area, then Left. Type a note. Tap "Stop this exercise".
5. Done sets stay; the next exercise starts.
6. Open it again, tap "End the session". Headline: "Stopping was the right call. Your sets are saved."
7. Card "Noted today" lists your note, once.
8. Train header shows the account save state, "Synced".
9. Start a session, log two sets, End, Discard. One tap, nothing in Recent.

## 2. Airplane mode
10. Airplane on. Start, open the sheet, Stop. Report says "Saved on this device".
11. Airplane off. It turns "Synced", once.
12. Two fast taps on Stop: "Noted today" shows one note.

## 3. Lock
13. Open the sheet during rest, lock two minutes, unlock. Clock right, sheet still works.

## 4. Kill the tab
14. Stop, close the tab, reopen. Note and sets still there.

## 5. 320 px
15. Zoom to 320 px. Sheet, area chips, Done card: no sideways scroll.

## 6. Night Gym
16. Dark mode. Sheet and Done follow; back to light, same.
